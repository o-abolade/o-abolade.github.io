Academic journey

I study Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. My academic path has moved from foundational engineering into computer vision, learning-based perception, and robotics.

Across research and project work, I have explored how machines interpret visual and multimodal information, understand depth and three-dimensional structure, and use that understanding in autonomous and embodied settings.

I am especially interested in systems that connect perception with reasoning: models that do more than recognize a scene and can support intelligent action in the physical world.
