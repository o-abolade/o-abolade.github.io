Personal project

Xolem

An investing autopilot that can operate as a self-sufficient baseline or expand around an investor’s own ideas.

FocusInvestment autopilot · Portfolio construction · Personal idea augmentation

TimeframeJul. 2026–Present

Xolem is a private investing autopilot for people who want access to a more active investment process without needing to become traders, quants, or macro researchers. Its baseline path keeps a coherent portfolio working on their behalf, monitoring its environment and evaluating opportunities within defined decision logic.

It can also augment an investor who has a thesis, a market observation, or a high-conviction idea worth investigating. Xolem brings that input together with portfolio context, research signals, and risk parameters so an opportunity can be assessed, sized, and revisited with a clear rationale.

Two ways to use the autopilot

The baseline offers a complete starting point; personal ideas can extend the same system when an investor wants to be more involved.

Personal conviction becomes a structured input—not an isolated bet detached from the rest of the portfolio.

Both paths keep the link between a decision, its portfolio role, and its eventual outcome intact.

Markets are dynamic and heterogeneous: a compelling idea can have real upside, while returns can also be path-dependent and fragile. Xolem’s aim is not to replicate a broker’s basic automation or a conventional quant workflow. It is to build an investing autopilot that can help pursue opportunities, incorporate personal conviction when useful, and keep risk, uncertainty, and later review part of the decision itself.

This is a private personal project and not investment advice. The public overview intentionally omits strategy logic, model design, execution mechanics, broker integrations, and account-specific information.
