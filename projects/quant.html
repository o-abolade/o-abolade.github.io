Personal project

Investment Autopilot Harness

An investment decision system that turns external information and quantitative evaluation into evidence-tracked, approval-gated portfolio operations.

FocusDecision systems · Portfolio policy · Risk controls

TimeframeJul. 2026–Present

The Investment Autopilot Harness is a private decision system for turning external information and quantitative research into disciplined, reviewable portfolio operations. It combines quantitative methods with explicit portfolio policy, risk constraints, and a durable record of the evidence behind each decision.

Rather than treating automation as a black box, the system is organized around a clear operating loop: evaluate proposals, apply constraints, review decisions for approval, and preserve the rationale and outcomes for later analysis.

High-level operating model

01

External information

Market data · Public filings · News · Economic context

02

Research & evaluation

Quantitative analysis · Evidence tracking · Scenario review

03

Portfolio policy

Risk limits · Exposure constraints · Decision criteria

04

Approval-gated operations

Proposal review · Explicit approval · Execution handoff

05

Audit & learning loop

Decision record · Outcome review · Policy refinement

Each stage produces a reviewable record so that decisions, constraints, approvals, and outcomes remain traceable.

The project explores how quantitative research, portfolio policy, and software architecture can work together in a practical investing workflow. The diagram is intentionally shown at a portfolio level; implementation, data sources, research methods, and account-specific details remain private while the system develops.

This public overview does not disclose strategy logic, model design, execution mechanics, broker integrations, or account-specific information.
