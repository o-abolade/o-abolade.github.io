Quant Research Harness

A private autopilot-style trading harness designed for live brokerage workflows, with decision, risk, and execution discipline built in.

FocusDecision systems · Portfolio operations · Risk controls

StatusPrivate, in development

The Quant Research Harness is designed around a simple discipline: record an investment thesis before the outcome is known, state what would falsify it, and retain the evidence needed to evaluate that decision later.

It combines portfolio construction, risk checks, and an auditable decision record into an autopilot-style workflow for live brokerage operations. The intent is to make every action traceable back to a stated thesis and bounded by explicit constraints, rather than treating automation as a substitute for evidence.

The repository, data, research methods, and account-specific details remain private while the system evolves. This page is intentionally a portfolio-level overview.
