Investment Autopilot Harness

A private investment-autopilot harness that combines quantitative methods, evidence-tracked proposals, and approval-gated execution.

FocusDecision systems · Portfolio policy · Risk controls

StatusPrivate, in development

The Investment Autopilot Harness has evolved beyond a quant-research-only workflow into a broader decision system for personal and managed investment autopilot use cases. Quantitative research remains a core part of the system.

It is designed to turn an investment idea into an evidence-tracked, risk-bounded proposal: a stated thesis, relevant constraints, a clear review path, and an auditable record. Quantitative methods and automation support research, portfolio policy, and execution readiness, while broker actions remain approval-gated.

The repository, data, research methods, and account-specific details remain private while the system evolves. This page is intentionally a portfolio-level overview.
