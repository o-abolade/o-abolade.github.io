Personal project

Xolem

A private investing autopilot for developing, testing, and scaling differentiated investment ideas through structured research, portfolio context, and explicit decision records.

FocusInvestment autopilot · Portfolio construction · Decision systems

TimeframeJul. 2026–Present

Xolem is a private investing autopilot designed for investors who want to pursue differentiated opportunities, rather than simply automate a conventional portfolio. It brings investment theses, research signals, portfolio context, and risk parameters into one decision system so each opportunity can be assessed, sized, and revisited with a clear rationale.

Markets are dynamic and heterogeneous: a compelling idea can have real upside, while returns can also be path-dependent and fragile. Xolem is designed to preserve both sides of that reality—creating room for high-conviction opportunities while making the assumptions, constraints, and outcomes behind each decision visible.

Each stage keeps the link between an idea, its assumptions, its portfolio role, and its eventual outcome intact.

Xolem’s aim is not to replicate a broker’s basic automation or a conventional quant workflow. It is to build a more thoughtful investing autopilot: one that can help surface, test, and scale investment ideas while keeping risk, uncertainty, and later review part of the decision itself.

This is a private personal project and not investment advice. The public overview intentionally omits strategy logic, model design, execution mechanics, broker integrations, and account-specific information.
