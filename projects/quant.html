Quant Research Harness

A private decision-measurement and portfolio-research system for testing whether investment judgment holds up over time.

FocusDecision systems · Portfolio research · Risk controls

StatusPrivate, in development

The Quant Research Harness is designed around a simple discipline: record an investment thesis before the outcome is known, state what would falsify it, and retain the evidence needed to evaluate that decision later.

It combines a research and paper-evaluation layer with portfolio construction, risk checks, and an auditable decision record. The point is not to present a strategy as proven; it is to build an instrument that can distinguish a repeatable signal from hindsight, noise, or an unsupported intuition.

The repository, data, research methods, and account-specific details remain private while the system evolves. This page is intentionally a portfolio-level overview.
