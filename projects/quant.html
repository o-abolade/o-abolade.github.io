Personal project

Xolem

A private investing-autopilot harness that turns ideas into evidence-tracked, bounded forward experiments and reviewable portfolio decisions.

FocusInvestment research systems · Decision provenance · Risk-gated virtual portfolios

TimeframeJul. 2026–Present

Xolem is a private, operator-supervised investment research and control system. It provides a structured path from a thesis and source context to an explicit decision, a bounded portfolio proposal, and a retained record of what happened afterward.

Recent work has added a baseline-first workspace for examining proposals, versioned candidate policies, and separate forward experiments. Candidate decisions are recorded before outcomes, compared with matched baseline and hold controls, and retained for review; they do not automatically alter the baseline or gain execution authority.

The project focuses on building an accountable operating layer around personal investment research—not claiming a proven investment model or autonomous execution. Strategy logic, account data, broker integrations, and private evaluation details remain undisclosed.

This is a private personal project and not investment advice. The public overview does not disclose strategy logic, model design, execution mechanics, broker integrations, or account-specific information.
