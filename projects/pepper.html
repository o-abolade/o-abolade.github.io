Pepper Exercise Coach

A socially assistive robotics project focused on guided exercise experiences.

FocusHuman-robot interaction · Robotics

StatusSelected project

Pepper Exercise Coach explores the use of a social robot in an exercise-coaching context. The work brings together robot behavior, interaction design, and the practical challenges of making a guided experience feel supportive and understandable.

The project is part of a broader interest in robots that engage with people in real environments, rather than operating only in tightly constrained technical settings.
