3D Reconstruction & Computer Vision

Completed work in visual understanding, scene geometry, and image-based reconstruction.

FocusComputer vision · 3D geometry

StatusCompleted work

This collection of work centered on the computer-vision problems behind recovering structure from images and reasoning about scenes in three dimensions.

It provided an early technical foundation for later interests in depth estimation, multimodal perception, and robotic systems that need to understand the geometry of their environment.
