Personal project

Robotic Reasoning Models for Physical AI

RRM-1 is a private personal project for robot-agnostic embodied reasoning, connecting persistent world modeling, structured task planning, independent supervision, and embodiment-aware execution.

FocusRobot-agnostic reasoning · World modeling · Physical AI

TimeframeAug. 2026–Present

StatusPrivate work · In active development

RRM-1 is designed to make robot behavior inspectable across the full task loop. Rather than sending a high-level goal directly to a policy, it maintains structured state about objects, relations, actions, and expected effects, then checks proposed actions before execution.

Current work emphasizes explicit contracts and replayable evidence: versioned state, bounded planning calls, admission checks, safety gates, and monitored execution traces. The architecture is designed to evaluate pluggable models, reasoning modules, and embodiment adapters through stable interfaces rather than binding the system to one model or robot.
