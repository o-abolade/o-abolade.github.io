Robotic Reasoning Models for Physical AI

A research prototype for reliable task reasoning in embodied systems.

FocusEmbodied AI · Robotics

StatusPrivate research prototype

RRM explores a modular approach to robot task reasoning. It connects semantic scene understanding, planning, and independent verification so an embodied system can reason about what it should do before it acts.

The central question is whether explicit world modeling and verification can make robotic behavior more robust, recoverable, and safety-aware than purely end-to-end control.

This is an intentionally high-level public overview. The implementation, experimental setup, and current results remain private while the work develops.
