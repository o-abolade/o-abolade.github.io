Research focus

Multimodal Passive Depth Perception for Autonomous Navigation

Investigating how visual and multimodal cues can give autonomous systems a useful understanding of space without relying on active depth sensors.

FocusComputer vision · Robotic perception · Autonomy

StatusResearch direction

Reliable navigation depends on knowing not only what is present in a scene, but where it is and how it relates to an agent's possible motion. This research direction investigates ways to infer that spatial structure from passively observed visual and multimodal signals.

The work connects depth estimation with practical autonomy: representing nearby geometry, reasoning about uncertainty, and producing perception outputs that can be used by a navigation system rather than treated as an endpoint by themselves.

It also reflects a broader research interest in robust perception under real-world constraints, where sensing can be incomplete, environments can change, and a system must still make useful decisions.
