Research focus

Multimodal Passive Depth Perception for Autonomous Navigation

Investigating how multimodal perception can support spatial understanding without active depth sensing.

FocusComputer vision · Autonomy

StatusResearch direction

This research direction considers how visual and multimodal signals can contribute to depth understanding in settings where an autonomous system must perceive its surroundings and navigate reliably.

It sits at the intersection of learning-based perception, scene geometry, and autonomy: translating sensory observations into a useful representation of space for downstream decision-making.
